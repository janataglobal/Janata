Quality Standards

Our Quality Inspection Process

Every shipment from Janata Global Export undergoes a structured quality verification process to ensure products meet international buyer requirements and applicable standards.

📋

Specification Review

Before sourcing begins, we review all buyer specifications: diameter, gauge, zinc coating, tensile strength, grade, applicable standard (ASTM/BS/IS), and packaging requirement. No ambiguity, no surprises.

🏭

Verified Manufacturer Sourcing

We source only from Indian manufacturers with verified production capabilities and documented quality management systems. Manufacturer selection is based on product type and specification match.

🔬

Mill Test Certificate (MTC)

Mill Test Certificates are obtained for all steel and metal products confirming chemical composition and mechanical properties. Provided as standard for most product lines.

📏

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Dimensional checks, weight verification, coating thickness measurements and visual inspection are performed before packing. Third-party inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas or similar can be arranged on buyer request.

📦

Packaging Standards

Products are packed to buyer specifications and destination requirements: coil, spool, pallet, bundle or drum. Export-grade packaging materials used for all ocean freight shipments.

📑

Complete Documentation Set

We provide a full commercial document set: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (COO), Bill of Lading, Mill Test Certificate, and any additional certificates required by the destination country.

Global Logistics

Port-to-Port Export Logistics from India

We coordinate the complete export logistics process — from factory to your destination port — using India's top shipping ports and established freight partners.

01

FCL / LCL Booking

Full Container Load (FCL) or Less than Container Load (LCL) — depending on your order volume, we select the most cost-effective shipping option.

02

Factory-to-Port Transport

Goods are transported from the Indian manufacturer to the designated port (Mundra / JNPT / Vizag) by our logistics partners.

03

Containerization & Stuffing

Goods are packed, weighed and containerized at CFS or factory. Stuffing is supervised to ensure damage-free loading.

04

Customs Clearance & Filing

Export customs documentation is filed using our IEC code. Shipping Bill, Export Declaration and all required forms processed by our CHA.

05

B/L & Shipping Docs

Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, COO and MTC are sent to buyer within 5–7 working days of vessel departure.

06

Destination Port Arrival

Container is tracked and ETA provided. We support buyer's customs clearance process with all necessary supplementary documentation.

⚓

Mundra Port

India's largest private port. Primary dispatch point for GCC and Gulf destinations. Short transit time to UAE and Saudi Arabia.

🚢

JNPT (Nhava Sheva)

Jawaharlal Nehru Port, Mumbai — India's busiest container port. Excellent connectivity to global shipping lines for worldwide destinations.

🏗️

Vizag Port

Visakhapatnam Port — close to our Hyderabad base. Ideal for bulk steel and metal cargo. Strong connectivity to East Africa and South Asia.

Incoterms & Pricing

Available Trade Terms (Incoterms 2020)

We support multiple Incoterms to suit your procurement model. Select the option that best fits your import workflow.

FOB

Free On Board

Seller delivers goods on board the vessel at the named Indian port. Buyer takes responsibility from that point. Most common for experienced importers.

CIF

Cost, Insurance & Freight

Seller covers cost, insurance and freight to destination port. Buyer handles import clearance and delivery. Popular for Gulf importers.

CFR

Cost & Freight

Like CIF but without insurance. Seller pays freight, buyer arranges cargo insurance independently. Flexible option for buyers with preferred insurers.

EXW

Ex Works

Buyer collects from the factory. Seller has minimum obligations. Suitable for buyers with their own freight forwarders in India.

DAP

Delivered At Place

Seller delivers goods at a named destination. Buyer handles import duties. Available on request for select destinations.

Our team will guide you on the best Incoterm for your destination and import setup.
