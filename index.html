Our Process

A Structured Path from Enquiry to Export

From your first enquiry to container dispatch, we manage every step with professionalism and transparency.

01

Share Your Requirement

Provide product specification, quantity (MT), destination port and any technical requirements via our RFQ form or WhatsApp.

02

Specification Review

Our team reviews the technical details — gauge, coating, grade, packaging, required standards — and clarifies any gaps.

03

Supplier Sourcing & Pricing

We identify the best Indian manufacturers, obtain competitive pricing and available mill test certificates.

04

Quotation & Documentation

Formal export quotation with Incoterms (FOB/CIF/CFR), complete with all required trade documentation.

05

Order & Containerization

Production or stock is coordinated, quality inspected, and goods are packed & containerized to specification.

06

Shipment & Export Delivery

Container dispatched from Indian port. All shipping documents (B/L, COO, Packing List, Invoice) provided on time.
