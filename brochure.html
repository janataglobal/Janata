Company Overview

Janata Global Export is an IEC-licensed, GST-registered B2B export company headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. We specialize in the export of high-grade GI Wire, Wired Nails, Aluminium Sections, Steel Coils, Rebar and related metal products to international buyers across the Gulf, GCC, Middle East, Africa and worldwide markets. All products are supplied to ASTM, BS and IS standards with complete documentation.
